🕊️ Glider Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

In short: Glider has no servers, no analytics and no ads. Files you give it travel directly between your Mac and your own Dropbox account. We never see your files, your Dropbox account or your sign-in.

1. Overview

OsiVibeCode LLC, an Arizona limited liability company, publishes Glider, a macOS utility that uploads files you drop on it to your Dropbox, and can keep one folder on your Mac in sync with Dropbox. This policy explains what the app reads, what it sends and to whom, and what it stores. Glider has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

2. Your Dropbox Account

  • Sign-in: happens in your browser, on dropbox.com. Glider never sees your Dropbox password. Dropbox gives Glider a token, which it keeps in your Mac's Keychain.
  • Access: Glider is a Dropbox "App folder" app: it can only read and write inside Apps › GliderApp in your Dropbox, never the rest of it.
  • Account details: Glider reads your Dropbox account's name, email address and account ID, to show which account is which.
  • Disconnecting: Remove Account in Glider revokes its access. You can also disconnect Glider at any time on dropbox.com › Settings › Connected apps.

3. Your Files

  • Uploads: only files and folders you choose: dropped on Glider, picked in its file dialog, sent with “Upload with Glider”, or placed in a folder you chose for Glider. They go directly from your Mac to Dropbox over an encrypted connection.
  • Sync: if you turn on two-way sync for a folder, Glider also downloads files from Apps › GliderApp into that folder, and mirrors deletions in both directions. Deleted files go to your Mac's Trash and remain restorable in Dropbox.
  • Glider never reads file contents for any purpose other than sending them to Dropbox and checking they arrived intact (a checksum Dropbox also computes).

4. Data Stored Locally

Glider keeps the following on your Mac. None of it is sent to us:

  • Keychain: Dropbox sign-in tokens, one per account.
  • Accounts: each account's name, email, Dropbox folder and Glider folder settings.
  • Activity and sync state: the names, paths, sizes and dates of files you uploaded or synced, the last 100 uploads, and which version of each synced file is where, so nothing uploads twice.
  • Settings: in ~/Library/Preferences/com.osivibecode.glider.plist.

Everything except the Keychain items lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Glider/. Glider's own logs never contain sign-in tokens or file contents, and file names in them are hidden by macOS unless you are the Mac's administrator debugging it.

5. Network Connections

Glider connects only to Dropbox (dropbox.com, api.dropboxapi.com, content.dropboxapi.com and notify.dropboxapi.com), to sign in, upload, download and learn about changes. Dropbox handles your files under its own privacy policy. Glider does not check for updates and contacts no server of ours.

6. Third-Party Services

The only outside service is Dropbox, which you choose to connect. Glider contains no advertising SDK, no analytics library and no other cloud service. Glider is not made by or affiliated with Dropbox.

7. Permissions

  • Notifications — optional, to say when uploads finish.
  • Open at login — optional. Managed by macOS in System Settings › General › Login Items.
  • Files and folders — only the ones you give Glider.

Glider does not ask for Accessibility, Screen Recording, Full Disk Access, your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove everything Glider stored:

  • Remove each account in Glider (this also revokes its Dropbox access).
  • Delete ~/Library/Application Support/Glider/ and ~/Library/Preferences/com.osivibecode.glider.plist.
  • Move Glider to the Trash.

Your files in Dropbox and in your Glider folders are yours and stay where they are.

9. Children's Privacy

Glider is a general-purpose desktop utility and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of Glider ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
