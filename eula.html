4. What the Software Does

Glider uploads the files you choose to your Dropbox account and, if you turn it on, keeps one folder per account in two-way sync with Dropbox through the Dropbox API. Your use of Dropbox remains subject to Dropbox's own terms. Glider is not made by or affiliated with Dropbox.

Sync changes files on both sides. With two-way sync on, deleting or changing a file in the synced folder deletes or changes it in Dropbox, and the other way round. Glider sends local deletions to the Trash and Dropbox keeps deleted files restorable for its retention period, and Glider asks before deleting many files at once — but keep your own backups of anything important. Choosing “Move” moves your original files into the Glider folder.
